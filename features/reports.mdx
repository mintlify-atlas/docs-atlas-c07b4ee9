## Overview

The Reports module provides comprehensive analytics and KPIs for management decision-making. It includes executive dashboards, productivity reports with deviation analysis, and operational metrics across work orders, resources, and materials.

## Dashboard KPIs Explained

### Production Status KPIs

<ParamField path="kpis.openCount" type="integer">
  Total work orders currently open (not delivered/cancelled)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="kpis.inProcessCount" type="integer">
  Work orders actively in production (status: `EN_PROCESO`)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="kpis.finishedCount" type="integer">
  Work orders completed in selected period (status: `FINALIZADA` or `ENTREGADA`)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="kpis.delayedCount" type="integer">
  Work orders past their commitment date
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="kpis.hoursToday" type="decimal">
  Total hours worked today across all operators
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="kpis.operatorsActive" type="integer">
  Number of operators currently assigned to work orders
</ParamField>

### Financial KPIs

<ParamField path="managementAnnual.revenueEstimated" type="decimal">
  Total estimated revenue from closed work orders
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="managementAnnual.materialCostReal" type="decimal">
  Actual material costs consumed (from MaterialConsumption records)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="managementAnnual.gainEstimated" type="decimal">
  Estimated profit from work orders completed under budget
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="managementAnnual.lossEstimated" type="decimal">
  Estimated loss from work orders over budget
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="managementAnnual.marginEstimated" type="decimal">
  Net margin: gain minus loss
</ParamField>

### Alerts

<ParamField path="kpis.alerts.total" type="integer">
  Total deviation alerts (warning + critical)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="kpis.alerts.warning" type="integer">
  Work orders exceeding warning thresholds
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="kpis.alerts.critical" type="integer">
  Work orders exceeding critical thresholds
</ParamField>

## Productivity Report Metrics

### Deviation Analysis

The system calculates time and cost deviations for each work order:

**Time Deviation:**
```
timeDeviationHours = workedHours - estimatedHours
timeDeviationPct = (timeDeviationHours / estimatedHours) × 100
```

**Cost Deviation:**
```
costDeviation = realMaterialCost - estimatedCost
costDeviationPct = (costDeviation / estimatedCost) × 100
```

### Alert Severity Levels

<Steps>
  <Step title="ok">
    Within thresholds:
    - Time deviation < 20%
    - Cost deviation < 15%
  </Step>
  <Step title="warning">
    Moderate deviation:
    - Time deviation 20-40%
    - Cost deviation 15-30%
  </Step>
  <Step title="critical">
    Severe deviation:
    - Time deviation > 40%
    - Cost deviation > 30%
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Worked Hours Calculation

Worked hours are computed from `OperationLog` events:
- Sum time between `INICIO` and `FINALIZACION` events
- Exclude time during `PAUSA` periods
- Convert minutes to hours (divide by 60)

## Period Selection

The dashboard supports 3 time periods:

| Period | Months | Use Case |
|--------|--------|----------|
| 3M | 3 months | Short-term trends, quarterly review |
| 6M | 6 months | Mid-term analysis, semester performance |
| 12M | 12 months | Annual overview, year-over-year comparison |

Period selection affects:
- `managementAnnual` totals
- `monthlyManagement` array length
- Chart data displayed

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="KPI Monitoring">
    **Daily checks:**
    - Delayed count (address overdue orders)
    - Critical alerts (investigate severe deviations)
    - In-process count (manage workload)
    
    **Weekly review:**
    - Average hours per OT (identify efficiency trends)
    - Operator load distribution (balance assignments)
    - Material cost trends (catch cost increases early)
    
    **Monthly analysis:**
    - Financial margins (profitability assessment)
    - Productivity deviations (process improvements)
    - Status distribution (workflow optimization)
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Deviation Management">
    **When critical alerts appear:**
    1. Review work order details in depth
    2. Analyze operation logs for delays/pauses
    3. Compare material consumption vs. estimates
    4. Document lessons learned
    5. Adjust future quotations accordingly
    
    **Threshold tuning:**
    - Warning thresholds: 20% time, 15% cost (default)
    - Critical thresholds: 40% time, 30% cost (default)
    - Adjust based on your industry standards
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Chart Interpretation">
    **Line charts:**
    - Rising avg hours/OT → complexity increasing
    - Declining closed orders → capacity issues
    
    **Bar charts:**
    - Revenue > material cost → healthy margin
    - Material cost approaching revenue → pricing review needed
    
    **Pie charts:**
    - High "Pausada" percentage → investigate bottlenecks
    - Low "Entregada" → delivery process issues
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
