## Your Role in the System

As a supervisor, you are the **operational coordinator** of the workshop. Your job is:

✅ Distribute work among operators  
✅ Track in real-time what's happening on the floor  
✅ Detect and resolve delays before they reach the client  
✅ Register workshop resources and materials

## Main Screen: The Dashboard

When you log in, you see the **Management Dashboard** with metrics for the selected period.

### Main KPIs

- **Open orders**: Total active work orders in the system
- **In process now**: Work orders with operators currently working
- **Overdue**: Work orders past their delivery date without closing ⚠️
- **Closed**: Work orders completed in the period (3M / 6M / 12M)
- **Avg. time/WO**: Average hours per order
- **Active operators**: How many are working this shift

### Period Filter

Use the **3M / 6M / 12M** buttons to view data for the last quarter, semester, or year.

### Charts

- **Production trend**: Closed work orders + average hours per month
- **Order status**: Donut chart with distribution by status
- **Revenue vs Cost**: Estimated revenue vs actual materials cost
- **Monthly margin**: Profit/loss month by month

### Alerts

If there are work orders with time or cost deviations, they appear in the **Operational Alerts** section with two levels:
- 🟡 **WARNING**: Close to the limit
- 🔴 **CRITICAL**: Exceeded threshold, needs immediate intervention

## Dispatch Center (Supervisor)

This is **the most important screen for daily work**.

Find it in the menu → **Dispatch**.

### What does it show?

**Shift KPIs (top row):**
- Work orders in process / Paused / Overdue / Active operators

**Work orders in execution:**
- Each card shows the active work order with:
  - Code + title + client
  - Status (color: green=in process, orange=paused, red=overdue)
  - Assigned operators and resources
  - Delivery date

**Operators on shift:**
- List of who is working on which work order

**Pending queue:**
- Next 10 work orders to dispatch, sorted by priority

**Overdue table:**
- Overdue work orders with days delayed

<Note>
This screen automatically updates every 30 seconds.
</Note>

## Work Order Management

From **Orders** in the menu:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a work order">
    1. Click **New Work Order**
    2. Fill in: title, client, description, delivery date, priority, estimated hours
    3. You can link it to an approved budget
    4. Click **Create**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Assign operators and resources">
    1. Open the work order
    2. In the **Assigned Resources** section, select the operator or machine
    3. The operator will see the work order in their "My Shift" screen
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Change Work Order Status

Valid statuses and their flow:
```
PENDING → PLANNED → IN_PROCESS ⇄ PAUSED → COMPLETED → DELIVERED
```

You can change the status from the orders list using the status selector or from the detail view.

### Useful Filters

- By **status**: view only IN_PROCESS, PAUSED, etc.
- By **client**: search for a specific client's work orders
- By **operator**: view a person's workload

## Budget Management

From **Budgets**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and convert budgets">
    1. Create budget indicating client, items, and estimated values
    2. When client approves, convert it to a Work Order by clicking **Convert to WO**
    3. The work order is created with the budget data
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workshop Resources

From **Resources** you can view and manage:
- **Human resources**: operator name in the resources system (different from user)
- **Machines and tools**: lathes, milling machines, equipment, etc.

Resources are assigned to work orders to record which person or machine does each job.

## Materials

From **Materials** you can:
- View the materials catalog with unit price
- Add new materials
- View consumption history

## Productivity Reports

From **Reports**:
- Hours worked by operator
- Completed work orders
- Deviations between estimated and actual hours
- Cost analysis

## Daily Operation Tips

<Steps>
  <Step title="First thing in the morning">
    Check the Dispatch Center to see the workshop status
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="During the shift">
    Verify Dashboard alerts
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="At closing">
    Review the pending queue for the next day
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Weekly">
    Look at Productivity Reports
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Keep communication open with operators. The system tracks the "what" and "when," but you provide the "why" and "how."
</Note>
