<Info>
**For:** Workshop operators  
**Access:** Only the "My Shift" screen  
**What you can do:** View your assigned orders, record your work, and register materials you use
</Info>

## How to Log In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the system">
    Open the browser and enter the address your supervisor gave you (e.g., `http://192.168.1.10`)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter credentials">
    Enter your **email** and **password**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click login">
    Click **Login**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you forgot your password, ask the owner or supervisor to change it for you.
</Note>

## Main Screen — "My Shift"

When you log in, you see the **My Shift** screen directly.

Here you see **only the work orders assigned to you**.

Each order card shows:
- **Code** of the work order (e.g., `WO-0042`)
- **Title** of the job
- **Client** it's for
- **Current status** (colored badge)
- **Committed delivery date**
- **Action buttons**

## Work Order Statuses and Colors

| Status | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|----------|
| PLANNED | Blue | Work order assigned to you, not started yet |
| PENDING | Gray | Waiting for you to start |
| IN PROCESS | Green | You are working on it |
| PAUSED | Red | You stopped it temporarily |
| COMPLETED | Dark green | Work finished |

## Step by Step: How to Work a Work Order

### ▶ Step 1 — Start the Work Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your work order">
    Look for the work order in your list
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Verify status">
    Check that the status is **PLANNED** or **PENDING**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Start work">
    Click the **▶ Start** button
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm status change">
    The status changes to **IN PROCESS** (green) ✅
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
You can only start one work order at a time. If you already have one in process, the supervisor can reorganize them.
</Warning>

### ⏸ Step 2 — Pause (If You Need to Interrupt)

If you need to stop temporarily (lunch, lack of material, etc.):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate active work order">
    Find the work order that is **IN PROCESS**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Pause it">
    Click **⏸ Pause**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Status changes">
    The status changes to **PAUSED** (red)
  </Step>
</Steps>

To **resume** a paused work order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find paused work order">
    Look for it in your list (PAUSED status in red)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Resume work">
    Click **▶ Resume**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Status changes">
    The status returns to **IN PROCESS**
  </Step>
</Steps>

### ✅ Step 3 — Complete the Work Order

When you finish the work:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete work order">
    Click **✅ Complete**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add closing note">
    A confirmation window opens. You can add an optional **closing note** (e.g., "Completed according to specification")
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm completion">
    Confirm
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The system records the exact completion time. Make sure to complete only when the work is **actually** finished.
</Warning>

## Register Consumed Materials

While the work order is **IN PROCESS**, you can register which materials you used:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find consumption section">
    Look for the **Register consumption** section within the work order
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Search for material">
    Search for the material in the search box (e.g., "rod", "paint")
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter quantity">
    Enter the **quantity** used
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Register">
    Click **Register**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
This is very important because it allows the supervisor and owner to know the actual cost of the work.
</Note>

## Add Notes or Comments

You can leave notes about the work order at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find comment field">
    Look for the **Add update / comment** field
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Write your note">
    Write your note (e.g., "Missing material X, notified supervisor")
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add comment">
    Click **Add**
  </Step>
</Steps>

These notes are recorded with your name and exact date/time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't any work orders appear in my list?">
    The supervisor hasn't assigned you work yet. Check with them.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I see other operators' work orders?">
    No. You only see your assigned orders.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What if I made a mistake registering consumption?">
    Notify the supervisor so they can correct it from their screen.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Does the session close automatically?">
    Yes, after 8 hours you need to log in again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Quick Reference

| Task | Action |
|------|--------|

| Pause work | Click **⏸ Pause** on IN PROCESS work order |
| Resume work | Click **▶ Resume** on PAUSED work order |
| Complete work | Click **✅ Complete** on IN PROCESS work order |
